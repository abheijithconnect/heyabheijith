About

About I grew up in Kerala, studied economics and political science at Ramjas College in Delhi, and started out as a copywriter at Forest Creative Communications in Bengaluru. Economics taught me to read a market. Copywriting taught me to say one thing clearly. Building AI-powered websites and digital products taught me to take an idea all the way to something people use. I'm constantly taking in new information, new ways of answering a problem and new technology to build with.
