What I do

Most of my week sits somewhere between the brief and the final post. These are the parts I handle.

  • Brand positioningWorking out what a brand should stand for, and saying it simply.
  • Go-to-market (GTM)Launch strategy for new projects: audience, pricing story, channel plan and sales material.
  • MessagingOne clear story, adjusted for each audience.
  • Competitor researchSeeing what others in the category say, so we can say something else.
  • Client handlingRunning the day-to-day with founders and leadership teams.
  • CopywritingAds, brand lines, brochures, landing pages and mailers.
  • Content marketingContent calendars planned around what people are searching for.
  • Social mediaCampaign series, carousels and captions for Instagram and LinkedIn.
  • OOHHoardings, site branding and outdoor media that read in a glance.
  • Campaign ideasConcepts that can run for months, not a single post.
  • ScriptsShort films, walkthroughs and reels.
  • Website contentSite copy and page structure for real estate and consumer brands.
  • Landing pagesSingle-purpose pages written to turn a visit into an enquiry.
  • AI productsThree AI web prototypes, and AppPilot, an AI-led app store optimisation concept.
  • No-code buildsWorking prototypes and pages without waiting on a developer.
  • Prompt designPrompts and workflows that make AI useful for real marketing work.
  • Meta & Google adsBriefing campaigns and reading them for lead quality, not just volume.
  • SEO & AEOContent structured so search engines and AI answers pick it up.
  • AnalyticsGoogle Analytics and Looker Studio reports that a founder can read quickly.
  • CRM & outreachApollo, lead lists and follow-up sequences.
  • ReportingA weekly view of what is working and what to change.
  • Workflow setupBriefs, approvals and trackers so work moves without chasing.
  • Team managementDesigners, interns and freelancers working to one plan.
  • Vendor coordinationShoots, print and production partners.
  • AI toolsUsed for research and first drafts, with the final call made by a person.
  • Process designSimple systems that let a small team handle many brands.

About

I studied Economics and Political Science at Ramjas College, University of Delhi, and started in marketing as a copywriter at Forest Creative Communications in Bengaluru.

Today I am a brand strategist at Mediamix in Gurugram, handling brand communication for twelve clients. I work directly with founders on how their brand is positioned, launched and marketed, and I manage the team that makes it.

Most of my work is in real estate, from ultra-luxury launches to senior living. I also work across healthcare, FMCG, travel and interiors.

Outside campaigns, I write website content and build small AI products.
