01  /  How I work

Three things have to be true before a campaign is worth running.

i

The brand knows what it is

Someone has to decide what a brand stands for and, harder, what it will stop chasing. I sit in that conversation with founders and leadership before anything goes to design.

12 brands under my communication at Mediamix. Real estate, healthcare, FMCG, travel, architecture, interiors.

ii

The market hears it the same way

Positioning is a theory until launch day. I take it through pricing narrative, channel plan, sales collateral and media, so the story a buyer hears matches the one we wrote.

Co-led go-to-market for three premium residential launches, from the first pitch line to the closing report.

iii

The work can actually ship

Good thinking dies in bad process. I build the system that gets work out of the door: briefs that hold, one source of truth, a review rhythm people keep.

Rebuilt campaign workflow at Mediamix and cut delivery time to a third. Run a team of eight.

04  /  About

I came into marketing through economics, and it shows.

I studied Economics and Political Science at Ramjas College, University of Delhi. It is a strange route into brand work and it turned out to be a useful one. I start with the question underneath the brief: what are we selling, who is genuinely in the market this quarter, and what would have to change for them to move.

Right now I am at Mediamix in Gurugram, holding communication for twelve brands. Most days that means being the person the client calls first and the person the creative team argues with. Before that I was at Forest Creative Communications in Bengaluru, writing across healthcare and real estate, which is where I learned to write fast and edit harder.

Real estate is where I have gone deepest. It is a category where one decision costs a buyer years of income, so the communication has to be exact. I like that pressure.
