Back to work Case 03

Silverglades, The Legacy

At thirteen crore, nobody wants to be sold to.

Sector
Luxury residential
Location
Sector 63A, Gurugram
Role
Strategic communication, copy
At
Mediamix
The problem

Silverglades has been building addresses in Gurugram for three decades, and they call themselves The Address Makers, which is a claim you have to keep earning. The Legacy sells between 7.35 and 13.55 crore. At that price a feature list does nothing. Nobody buying a four bedroom at thirteen crore needs to be told it has four bedrooms.

The move

Write the buyer, not the building. At this end of the market the reader is not being convinced of anything, they are checking whether they recognise themselves. So each line is a mirror and the apartment turns up afterwards as evidence.

Sculpted for those who see beyond the skyline.

Same rule on the second execution. Where thought leadership resides describes a person and their study, and lets the penthouse be the proof rather than the pitch. The room does the selling. The copy just gives the reader permission to want it.

How it runs

I hold this account with the leadership team rather than through a brief chain, which means most of these calls get made in the room and not in a deck three days later. That changes what the work can be. You can argue for a quieter line when the person who signs it is sitting there while you argue for it.
