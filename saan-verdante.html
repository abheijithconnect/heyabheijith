Back to work Case 02

Saan Verdante

Build the name into the view.

Sector
Boutique residential
Location
Sector 95, Gurugram
Role
Launch identity, campaign, performance copy
At
Mediamix
The problem

Saan Verdante is a terraced-hill tower where every floor gets its own private garden, right to the top. That is genuinely unusual in Gurgaon, and it is also the kind of thing that disappears in a feature list. The product is the view. The brand had to be readable before anyone reached a paragraph.

The move

We built the launch identity out of the letterforms themselves. S, A, A and N were filled with the terraces, the water, the towers at dusk and the interiors, so the name is not sitting next to the product, it is made of it.

If the name carries the view, you only have to be seen once.

Each letter took one promise: serene beginnings, abundance, nurtured living. Read across a phone screen it works as a single image. Read individually it works as three posts. That is the whole reason the grid holds together.

The other half

Brand work that cannot sell is decoration. The performance creative runs on a different logic to the identity work: it leads with the two things a Gurgaon buyer checks first, price and format, instead of burying them under a mood line. Four point three three crore, terrace garden apartment, first time in Gurgaon. The romance is in the identity. The numbers are in the ad.

Next case Silverglades, The Legacy At thirteen crore, nobody wants to be sold to.

If you have a brand that is almost right, that is the interesting part.
