About

I grew up in Kerala, studied Economics and Political Science at Ramjas College, University of Delhi, and found my way into brands through writing. My first role was as a copywriter at Forest Creative Communications, where every line had to earn its place.

Today I'm a brand strategist at Mediamix, working across twelve brands in real estate, healthcare, FMCG and travel. The work runs from positioning and go-to-market to the campaigns and content that carry them.

Alongside it, I design and build AI-powered websites and digital products, so an idea doesn't stop at the presentation. I'm constantly taking in new information, new ways of answering a problem and new technology to build with.
