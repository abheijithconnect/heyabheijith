CARE Hospitals

Say the price out loud.

Sector
Healthcare
Location
Hyderabad
Role
Content, patient collateral
At
Forest Creative Communications
The problem

Maternity is the most contested service line a hospital runs. It is also chosen by families who are anxious, comparing four hospitals at once, and reading everything twice. Most maternity marketing answers that anxiety with more reassurance, which is exactly the thing an anxious reader discounts.

The move

Persuasion was not the job. Clarity was. The Bloom suite collateral leads with what the package costs and what it does not cover, printed at the same size as the benefits. Normal delivery, C-section, exclusions, all of it on the front.

Printing the exclusions is the reason people believe the inclusions.

It sounds like a small production decision. It is the whole argument. A family that can see the number stops looking for the catch, and the rest of the page gets read properly.

The other half

The nutrition and weight guides do the job the ads cannot. They give the hospital something worth handing to a patient in a consultation, which is a different kind of marketing: useful on the day, and still on the fridge two months later. Written to be read by someone who is tired, so short sentences, real numbers, no euphemism.

Next case J Estates Later is not a plan.

If you have a brand that is almost right, that is the interesting part.
