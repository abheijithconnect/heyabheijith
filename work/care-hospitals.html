CARE Hospitals

Bloom Maternity

Patient material for a maternity programme in Hyderabad. Written for families who read every line and compare every price.

Client
CARE Hospitals, Nampally
Sector
Healthcare
Scope
Copywriting, print collateral
Agency
Forest Creative Communications

The brief

CARE Hospitals needed two pieces for expecting parents: a brochure for Bloom, its maternity suite packages, and a nutrition guide for pregnancy that doctors could hand out during consultations.

The approach

Families choosing where to deliver a baby are anxious, and they compare hospitals closely. Soft reassurance does not help much at that stage. Clear information does.

So the package brochure puts the price for a normal delivery and a C-section up front, lists what is included, and lists what is not, all at the same size. The nutrition guide keeps to short sections, real numbers and simple illustrations, so it is easy to read on a tired day and worth keeping afterwards.

What shipped

A tri-fold suite package brochure and a tri-fold pregnancy nutrition guide, both printed for use at the hospital's front desk and in consultation rooms.
