CARE Hospitals

Bloom Maternity

Two pieces of patient material for a hospital maternity programme. One helps a family choose; the other helps a mother act.

Client
CARE Hospitals, Nampally, Hyderabad
Pieces
Bloom suite packages brochure, pregnancy nutrition guide
My role
Copywriting
Agency
Forest Creative Communications

The problem

The two pieces had different jobs. The Bloom brochure sells a maternity suite package, a significant expense that a family will weigh carefully. The nutrition guide, for CARE Vatsalya Woman and Child Institute, gives medical guidance, where plain language matters and over-promising is not an option.

The decision

Make the decision easy to read, and the advice safe to follow.

The brochure gives both package prices in one block, ₹1,55,000 for a normal delivery and ₹1,85,000 for a C-section, and lists what is included and what is excluded on the same panel, so the real cost is clear at a glance. The guide turns clinical ranges into numbers a reader can use, and routes every recommendation back to a dietitian.

The lines

  1. Exclusions: admission, insurance and medical record charges.

    Printed on the same panel as the inclusions. Saying what is not covered, before anyone has to ask, is part of being trusted with a decision this size.

  2. Balanced weight gain helps your baby grow healthily while reducing pregnancy risks.

    The reason comes before the numbers, so the guidance reads as care rather than a rule.

  3. These are general guidelines. Every pregnancy is unique.

    The limits of the advice are stated in the same calm voice as the advice itself. Responsible medical writing that does not sound defensive.

  4. Get your personalised guide.

    Ends by turning a leaflet into a next step: a consultation with the hospital's dietitian.

The system

Short sections, real numbers and simple illustrations. Weight-gain guidance is given as a single target for each of three BMI bands rather than a paragraph of explanation. Both pieces carry the hospital address and appointment line, so every reader leaves with a way to act.

My role

Copywriting for both pieces.
