CARE Hospitals

Bloom Maternity

Patient material for a maternity programme in Hyderabad. Written for families who read every line and compare every price.

Client
CARE Hospitals, Nampally
Sector
Healthcare
Scope
Copywriting, print collateral
Agency
Forest Creative Communications

The brief

CARE Hospitals needed two pieces for expecting parents: a brochure for Bloom, its maternity suite packages, and a nutrition guide doctors could hand out during consultations.

The approach

Families choosing where to deliver a baby compare hospitals closely. Soft reassurance does not help at that stage. Clear information does.

The brochure puts the price, what is included and what is not up front, at the same size.

What shipped

A tri-fold suite package brochure and a tri-fold nutrition guide for the front desk and consultation rooms. Short sections, real numbers, simple illustrations.
