J Estates

J 36

A nine-part social campaign for a senior living project. Every safety feature, written as a way to live more freely.

Client
J Estates
Project
J 36, senior living
Scope
Campaign concept, copywriting
Agency
Mediamix

The brief

J 36 is J Estates' independent living project for seniors, designed around safety: grab bars, step-free ramps, anti-skid floors, rounded corners and a panic alarm. The brief was to show these features without making the home feel like a hospital.

The approach

Most senior living ads list safety features the way a care facility would, which tells an independent buyer they are being looked after.

Every post leads with the life and follows with the feature.

What shipped

Nine posts, one feature each, built as a set: a serif headline, a short rule, the feature in plain words, and warm interiors throughout.
