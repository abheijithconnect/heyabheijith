J Estates

J 36

A nine-part social campaign for a senior living project. Every safety feature, written as a way to live more freely.

Client
J Estates
Project
J 36, senior living
Scope
Campaign concept, copywriting
Agency
Mediamix

The brief

J 36 is J Estates' independent living project for seniors. The homes are designed around safety: grab bars, step-free ramps, anti-skid floors, rounded corners and a panic alarm in every home. The brief was to show these features to older buyers and their families without making the home feel like a hospital.

The approach

Most senior living ads list safety features the way a care facility would. That tells an independent sixty-five-year-old they are being looked after, which is exactly what they do not want to hear.

So every post leads with the life and follows with the feature. The headline is about freedom. The line under it names the safety detail in plain words. A railing, not a restriction. No threshold holds you back. Each one turns a precaution into something a buyer would want anyway.

What shipped

Nine posts, one feature each, built as a set: a serif headline, a short rule, the feature in plain words, and warm, lived-in interiors throughout. Together they cover the full list of safety features without ever reading like one.
