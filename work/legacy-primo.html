The Legacy Primo

A five-part social campaign for an ultra-luxury residential project in Gurugram. One real product fact per frame, said simply.

Client
Silverglades
Project
The Legacy Primo, Sector 63A
Scope
Campaign concept, copywriting
Agency
Mediamix

The brief

The Legacy Primo is the top-end phase of Silverglades' The Legacy: 4BHK residences of 4,800 sq ft each. The brief was a social campaign that made the project feel rare, without leaning on the usual luxury vocabulary.

The approach

Buyers at this level have already seen every adjective. What they have not seen is a clear list of what they actually get. So each post takes one real product fact and says it plainly: only two homes per floor, three sides open, a private clubhouse with its own infinity pool.

A short handwritten word sets the tone, a serif headline carries the fact, and the unit size sits in the same place on every frame. Seen together on the grid, the five posts read as one campaign.

What shipped

Five posts for Instagram and Facebook, built as a set: same frame, same type system, one idea each. The series gives the sales team five simple talking points that match what a buyer has already seen online.
